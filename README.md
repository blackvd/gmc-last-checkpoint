# Software Engineering Programme — Career Readiness Checkpoint

## My readiness for the next step

As I approach the end of GoMyCode’s Software Engineering programme, I feel ready to take the next step in my career. The bootcamp has strengthened the way I approach a project: I can break a problem into smaller tasks, build a solution, test it, and improve it based on feedback. I am confident about contributing to a development team, while recognising that I will keep learning on the job.

## Skills I developed

During the programme, I improved my skills in front-end and back-end development, working with databases, using Git and GitHub, debugging, and collaborating on projects. I also became more comfortable explaining my technical choices and managing my time when a task is challenging.

The most valuable lesson for me has been learning how to move from an idea to a working application. When I encounter a problem, I now take time to understand its cause, research possible solutions, and test my changes.

## What I would like to discuss with my tutor

I would appreciate feedback on the areas I should strengthen before pursuing my next opportunity, especially software architecture, code quality, testing, and technical interviews. I would also like advice on presenting my projects clearly in a portfolio and continuing to grow as a software engineer.

This programme has given me a stronger foundation and the confidence to keep building, learning, and taking on new responsibilities.
